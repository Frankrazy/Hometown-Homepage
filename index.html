Visit Lodwar, Turkana


Enjoy the vibrant and rich culture, traditions, and stunning natural landscapes.

Top five activities to do at Lodwar

1. Attend Tobong'u Lore Cultural Festival

Every year, Lodwar transforms into a massive global stage as indigenous pastoralist communities gather to celebrate their shared heritage through high-energy traditional dances, vibrant beaded regalia, and powerful cross-border peace dialogues. Welcoming you back home, this breathtaking exhibition of living history seamlessly blends vibrant artistic expression with vital regional diplomacy, successfully uniting the Ateker cluster nations from across East Africa.

2. Visit Lake Turkana & Central Island Excursins

As the ultimate gateway to the world’s largest permanent desert lake, Lodwar offers an unforgettable escape into the dramatic volcanic landscapes of Central Island National Park. Embark on a journey into the heart of the Jade Sea to explore everything from the white sand palm oases of Eliye Springs to the island's three distinct green crater lakes, which serve as a prehistoric paradise hosting the world's largest concentration of Nile crocodiles.

3. Lodwar Basket Market

Located right in the heart of the town center, Lodwar's bustling open-air craft market showcases the extraordinary resilience and artistry of local Turkana women. Discover the intricate art of Turkana basket weaving as you watch artisans dynamically hand-craft world-renowned, colourful dome baskets from wild doum palm fronds, while exploring endless rows of stalls packed with traditional beaded jewelry, handmade mats, and authentic local delicacies.

4. The "Rio de Janeiro of Africa" Statue

Perched boldly atop a 700-meter rocky hill overlooking the entire town, Lodwar’s striking Christ the Redeemer statue gives the town its nickname as the "Rio de Janeiro of Africa". Experience panoramic desert views and deep spiritual reflection by tackling the steep, meditative hiking path known as the "Way of the Cross". Built in 2004 by the local Catholic Diocese to mark a milestone of community evangelism and social service, this prominent 15-foot concrete monument rewards travelers with the most stunning sunset vistas of the town below and the distant Loima Hills

5. Premier Lodwar Hospitality & Stays

As Lodwar grows into a primary economic hub, the town has built a reliable network of premium hotels that seamlessly merge modern luxuries with authentic northern hospitality. Unwind in air-conditioned comfort and refresh at sparkling pool after a long day of exploring Turkana's unique landscapes. Whether you choose the luxury safari-style tents at The Cradle Tented Camp, the modern resort layout of Ten Villa Resort, or the vibrant culinary and pool scene at Stegra Hotel, you are guaranteed chilled amenities, locally sourced dining, and cold drinks right in Lodwar
